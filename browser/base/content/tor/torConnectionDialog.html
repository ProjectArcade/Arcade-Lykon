What is TOR?

TOR is a network that allows you to browse the internet anonymously. Your connection is routed through multiple servers, masking your IP address and location.

Benefits:
  • Browse anonymously and privately
  • Bypass geographic restrictions
  • Encrypted and secure communications
  • Extra layer of protection online
Important: TOR connection may be slower than regular browsing. While TOR masks your IP address, your browsing activity may still be logged by websites you visit. Use responsibly.
