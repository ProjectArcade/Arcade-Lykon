Garuda Vision Garuda Vision GarudaVision is a real-time, privacy-focused URL safety scanning engine built directly into the Lykon browser. It performs high-performance, sandboxed local checks on visited links to detect brand impersonation, phishing domains, subdomain abuse, and suspicious keyword patterns, keeping you safe from online scams without sharing your browsing history. General Protection Actively monitor page URLs and verify them against suspicious phishing heuristics. Disabling this will auto-hide the toolbar safety icon and turn off all background safety evaluations. Stop applying warning colors (red or yellow) to browser tabs when visiting dangerous or suspicious webpages. The toolbar warning indicators will still remain active. Allow security scanning and warning indicators to remain active when browsing inside private tabs or specialized Tor sessions. If disabled, all GarudaVision security scanning features will be hidden inside private browsing environments.
